Italian-born, Mexico-based software engineer with over five years of hands-on experience delivering production-grade software across robotics, automotive ADAS, and large-scale infrastructure. Currently at Grid Dynamics, diagnosing and resolving C++ defects in Google's TensorFlow core.

My sweet spot is the intersection of robotics, backend engineering, and data: modular ROS2 architectures with versioned interfaces and zero-downtime upgrade paths, cloud-native ETL pipelines (FastAPI + SQLAlchemy + Kafka + AWS), and ML-enhanced scoring/retraining loops (PyTorch, Scikit-learn). I'm comfortable taking a system from Gazebo simulation and planetary-analog challenges (ESA Space Resources, Leonardo Drone Contest - DRAFT PoliTO team) all the way to fleet-deployed, MISRA-aligned code.

Holder of a Master's in Mechatronic Engineering (2018-2021) and a Bachelor's in Aerospace Engineering (2014-2018) from Politecnico di Torino, with theses on model-predictive trajectory generation for four-wheel-steering UGVs and a Thales Alenia Space internship.

Italian native, with a passion for languages: English & Spanish fluent and Russian basic.
Remote-first, passport-ready, and always chasing the next hard problem in autonomous robotics.

Open to senior roles where I can own architecture and push real robots into the real world.
