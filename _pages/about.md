Italian-born, Mexico-based software engineer with over five years of hands-on experience delivering production-grade autonomy and automotive software at Ford, BMW, and a confidential multi-platform robotics program (UGV/UAV/UUV).

I design and ship real-time, safety-aware systems in modern C++ and Python: from low-level embedded firmware and Adaptive AUTOSAR serialization at BMW, to large-scale Python telemetry pipelines and HIL validation frameworks that powered Ford’s BlueCruise L2+ ADAS program, to leading the full-stack robotics architecture for a current senior role where I head an 8-engineer agile team.

My sweet spot is the intersection of robotics, backend engineering, and data: modular ROS2 architectures with versioned interfaces and zero-downtime upgrade paths, cloud-native ETL pipelines (FastAPI + SQLAlchemy + Kafka + AWS), and ML-enhanced scoring/retraining loops (PyTorch, Scikit-learn). I’m comfortable taking a system from Gazebo simulation and planetary-analog challenges (ESA Space Resources, Leonardo Drone Contest – DRAFT PoliTO team) all the way to fleet-deployed, MISRA-aligned code.

Holder of a Master’s in Mechatronic Engineering and a Bachelor’s in Aerospace Engineering from Politecnico di Torino, with theses on model-predictive trajectory generation for four-wheel-steering UGVs and a Thales Alenia Space internship.

Italian native, with passion for languages, with English & Spanish fluent, Chinese, Japanese and Russian basic.
Remote-first, passport-ready, and always chasing the next hard problem in autonomous robotics.

Currently looking for senior / lead opportunities where I can own architecture, mentor teams, and push real robots into the real world.
